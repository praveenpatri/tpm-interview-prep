# Candidate Profile Template

Fill this in internally from the resume and any other materials before writing the guide.
Leave a field blank if the materials don't support it. Do not guess.

## Basics
- Name (for the guide header only; omit contact details)
- Total years of experience / years as a TPM or program manager
- Current or most recent role, company, and dates
- Target level (from the posting, the user, or inferred; mark if inferred)

## Experience (most recent first)
For each role: company, title, dates, org or domain, team size, and **2-4 programs**:
- Program name or type
- Scope (teams, engineers, geographies, budget)
- Scale (users, requests, data volume, revenue, cost)
- Your role (owner, driver, influencer)
- Outcome with a metric if available

## Technical Depth
- Domains (e.g. data infrastructure, ML platform, mobile, cloud, security, devices)
- Systems and technologies named in the resume
- Where the candidate is hands-on vs. conversant

## Story Bank (candidate's best raw material)
Source these first from the stories and notes the user shares (Drive, upload, or paste), in whatever format they come. Fall back to resume programs.
Tag each program or story with the signals it can carry:
- Influencing without authority
- Ambiguity / zero-to-one
- Conflict or escalation
- Failure or recovery
- Risk management and tradeoffs
- Process or mechanism building
- Data-driven decision
- Cross-org or executive communication

## Thin Spots
Areas the target company is likely to probe where the resume shows little or no evidence.
